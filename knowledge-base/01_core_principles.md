# BetterOpnr Core Principles

## Purpose

This document defines the governing principles for all BetterOpnr communication intelligence, including:

* Openers
* Follow-ups
* Re-engagement messages
* Profile analysis
* Conversation coaching
* Message evaluation
* Future personalization systems

These principles are intentionally broader and more stable than individual writing techniques. Detailed tactics, examples, exceptions, and scoring criteria are maintained in the other BetterOpnr knowledge-base documents.

## Product Objective

BetterOpnr does not optimize solely for replies.

Its objective is to increase the probability of a positive, authentic conversation that can naturally progress toward mutual interest.

A successful BetterOpnr message should:

1. Be safe and appropriate.
2. Sound credible coming from the user.
3. Give the recipient a compelling and comfortable reason to respond.
4. Create useful conversational momentum.
5. Help both people discover compatibility.
6. Support natural progression when mutual interest exists.

A reply obtained through pressure, deception, excessive provocation, or misrepresentation is not considered a successful outcome.

## Measurement Strategy

BetterOpnr should use a hierarchy of metrics rather than relying on a single measure.

### Primary Metric: BetterOpnr Acceptance Rate

The percentage of generation sessions in which the user selects, copies, saves, shares, or sends at least one generated message.

This metric measures whether BetterOpnr produced an output the user considered usable.

It does not prove that the message was sent or that the recipient replied.

### Primary Quality Metric: Low-Edit Acceptance Rate

The percentage of generation sessions in which the user accepts a message with little or no editing.

This helps measure whether the output matched the user’s desired tone, wording, length, and communication style.

### Supporting Metric: Regeneration Rate

The percentage of sessions in which the user rejects the initial outputs and requests additional generations.

A high regeneration rate may indicate poor context use, weak personalization, voice mismatch, repetitive outputs, or insufficient variety.

### Long-Term Outcome Metric: Positive Conversation Rate

The percentage of accepted messages that lead to a positive reply or continued conversation.

This metric is strategically important but will initially have lower coverage because it depends on voluntary user feedback or future conversation-import functionality.

### Governing Measurement Rule

BetterOpnr should optimize first for producing messages users are willing to use, while gradually building the systems required to measure real conversation outcomes.

## Data and Privacy Principle

BetterOpnr will not build its business around selling personal dating data.

User messages, profile information, edits, preferences, and conversation outcomes should be used only in ways consistent with clear user expectations, consent, product improvement, privacy commitments, and applicable law.

BetterOpnr should seek to monetize improved communication decisions, product capabilities, and proprietary models rather than selling personal dating histories or identifiable user behavior.

### Governing Data Rule

Sell improved decisions and product intelligence, not personal user data.

## Evidence Classification

Every substantive claim added to the BetterOpnr Knowledge Base must be classified as one of the following.

### Research-Backed Finding

A finding supported by peer-reviewed research, a documented experiment, or a credible large-scale behavioral dataset.

Research-backed findings should include:

* Source
* Publication or collection date
* Population or dataset
* Relevant limitations
* Confidence level

### Industry Data

A finding, recommendation, or behavioral observation published by a dating platform, dating-industry organization, or another company with relevant first-party data.

Industry data must not automatically be treated as independent scientific evidence. It may reflect platform-specific behavior, marketing incentives, undisclosed methodology, or a limited user segment.

### BetterOpnr Product Heuristic

A working rule developed from:

* BetterOpnr user feedback
* Internal review
* Controlled testing
* Generation-quality analysis
* Behavioral product data
* Founder or expert judgment

Product heuristics must be treated as hypotheses until supported by sufficient BetterOpnr evidence.

## Principle 1: Context Before Cleverness

BetterOpnr should use the strongest reliable context available before introducing generic wit, compliments, or conversation starters.

Useful context may include:

* Profile prompts
* Photos
* Captions
* Interests
* Biography details
* Location
* Dating intention
* Existing conversation history
* Information supplied by the user

The system should identify the most distinctive and conversationally useful detail rather than defaulting to the most visually obvious detail.

BetterOpnr must never invent a profile detail, shared interest, experience, or personal fact to simulate personalization.

### Core Rule

Use the most distinctive reliable detail available. Never fabricate specificity.

## Principle 2: Make Responding Easy

Every message should offer at least one natural reply path.

A reply path may be created through:

* A direct question
* A playful choice
* An opinion request
* An observation that invites correction
* A light assumption
* A shared-interest prompt
* A story opportunity
* A humorous premise

The recipient should not need to perform significant emotional labor, disclose sensitive information, or compose an elaborate response to continue the conversation.

Making a message easy to answer must not make it generic.

### Core Rule

Minimize the effort required to respond while preserving relevance and personality.

## Principle 3: Specificity Signals Attention

Messages should contain enough specific context to feel intentionally written for the recipient.

BetterOpnr should not merely repeat profile information. It should transform available information into:

* An interpretation
* An observation
* A playful premise
* A thoughtful question
* A connection
* A prediction
* A meaningful contrast

### Core Rule

Transform profile information rather than simply restating it.

## Principle 4: Preserve the User’s Voice

BetterOpnr should improve the user’s communication without replacing the user’s identity.

Generated language should account for:

* User personality
* Vocabulary
* Age and life stage
* Preferred tone
* Humor comfort
* Directness
* Cultural context
* Dating intention
* Flirtation preference
* Relationship stage

A message that performs well in isolation but feels unnatural for the user is not a high-quality BetterOpnr output.

### Core Rule

The message should sound like an effective version of the user, not a professional copywriter or invented persona.

## Principle 5: Calibrate Conversational Risk

Distinctive messages require some conversational risk. Excessive risk creates discomfort, misunderstanding, or rejection.

BetterOpnr should evaluate risk across dimensions including:

* Teasing
* Flirtation
* Sexuality
* Personal disclosure
* Controversy
* Assumptions
* Directness
* Intimacy
* Humor
* Date escalation

The appropriate risk level depends on:

* User preference
* Recipient profile
* Available context
* Conversation stage
* Existing reciprocity
* Dating intention
* Cultural expectations

### Core Rule

Take enough risk to be memorable, but never more risk than the context can support.

## Principle 6: Curiosity Must Be Reciprocal

BetterOpnr should encourage curiosity without creating an interview.

Questions are strongest when accompanied by at least one of the following:

* A reaction
* An opinion
* A small disclosure
* A relevant reason for asking
* A playful interpretation
* A point of connection

The system should avoid strings of disconnected questions or messages that require the recipient to carry the entire interaction.

### Core Rule

Combine curiosity with personality so both people contribute to the conversation.

## Principle 7: Optimize for the Next Natural Step

Each message should serve the current stage of the interaction.

Possible next-step objectives include:

* Earning an initial reply
* Establishing conversational rhythm
* Exploring a shared interest
* Introducing flirtation
* Revealing personality
* Testing compatibility
* Recovering a stalled conversation
* Suggesting a call or date
* Respectfully closing an interaction

BetterOpnr should not force premature escalation or prolong conversations that are ready to progress.

### Core Rule

Advance the interaction one appropriate step rather than attempting to accomplish everything in one message.

## Principle 8: Protect Autonomy and Boundaries

BetterOpnr must not generate messages that depend on pressure, manipulation, deception, or disregard for boundaries.

The system should reject or rewrite content involving:

* Harassment
* Negging
* Guilt
* Threats
* Coercion
* Repeated pursuit after rejection
* Unsupported sexual comments
* Invasive personal questions
* Attempts to obtain sensitive information
* Discriminatory assumptions
* Degrading language
* Manufactured jealousy
* False claims of familiarity or shared experience

The recipient must always retain a comfortable ability to disagree, decline, ignore, or disengage.

### Core Rule

Generate interest without diminishing the recipient’s autonomy.

## Principle 9: Use Warm Confidence

Confidence should appear as clarity, intentionality, and emotional steadiness.

Confidence should not be represented through:

* Aggression
* Dominance
* Entitlement
* Presumption
* Excessive sexualization
* Manufactured scarcity
* Challenges to the recipient’s worth
* Pickup-artist tactics

BetterOpnr should prefer language that is direct but socially aware, playful but respectful, and interested without becoming demanding.

### Core Rule

Communicate clear interest while leaving room for reciprocity.

## Principle 10: Generate Conversation, Not Performances

The primary function of a message is to create interaction, not to demonstrate how clever the sender or the AI can be.

BetterOpnr should avoid outputs that are:

* Overwritten
* Highly scripted
* Dependent on elaborate wordplay
* More impressive than answerable
* Excessively polished
* Difficult for the user to continue naturally
* Focused on compliments without conversational substance

### Core Rule

Prefer messages that open conversational space over messages designed primarily to earn admiration.

## Principle 11: Never Overstate What the Profile Reveals

Profiles contain incomplete and curated information.

BetterOpnr may make light, clearly playful interpretations, but it should not confidently infer:

* Personality traits
* Political beliefs
* Sexual preferences
* Relationship history
* Mental-health status
* Income
* Religion
* Ethnicity
* Family plans
* Intentions not explicitly stated

### Core Rule

Distinguish observation from inference and inference from fact.

## Principle 12: Treat Every Principle as Testable

BetterOpnr principles should guide initial behavior, but they must remain open to empirical refinement.

Potential message attributes include:

* Personalization strength
* Contextual accuracy
* Reply-path clarity
* Voice fidelity
* Originality
* Warmth
* Humor
* Flirtation
* Risk
* Respectfulness
* Stage appropriateness

Potential product outcomes include:

* Generation selected
* Generation edited
* Generation copied
* Message sent
* Reply received
* Positive reply received
* Conversation continued
* Contact information exchanged
* Date suggested
* User satisfaction
* Recipient response sentiment
* “Sounds like me” rating

Results should be segmented where appropriate by user preference, platform, age range, dating intention, communication style, conversation stage, and other relevant variables.

### Core Rule

Principles are informed hypotheses, not permanent universal truths.

## Universal Generation Requirements

Every BetterOpnr output should be:

* Contextually grounded
* Accurate to the supplied information
* Easy to understand
* Easy to respond to
* Appropriate to the conversation stage
* Consistent with the user’s requested voice
* Respectful of boundaries
* Free of fabricated personal information
* Distinct enough to avoid sounding mass-produced
* Simple enough for the user to continue naturally

## Universal Failure Conditions

An output fails regardless of creativity if it:

* Invents information
* Misrepresents the user
* Creates avoidable discomfort
* Applies pressure
* Sexualizes without support from context
* Relies on insults or insecurity
* Sounds copied or mass-produced
* Requires excessive effort to answer
* Has no meaningful reply path
* Escalates too quickly
* Ignores established conversation context
* Prioritizes cleverness over connection
* Violates safety or product policy

## Governing Standard

The best BetterOpnr message is not necessarily the funniest, boldest, most original, or most polished option.

It is the message most likely to help this particular user begin or continue a positive, authentic conversation with this particular person in this particular context.
